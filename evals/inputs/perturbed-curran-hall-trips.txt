154 Curran Hall
City Wards: Scarborough Southwest – Ward 20, Scarborough Centre – Ward 21, Scarborough Guildwood – Ward 24, Scarborough-Rouge Park – Ward 25

Summary
The TTC is considering adding trips on the 154 Curran Hall in the weekday afternoon peak, to match capacity with demand. The route, its stops and its hours of service stay as they are, and no period of service is added or removed.

Rationale
Vehicle crowding on the 154 Curran Hall in the weekday afternoon peak has been consistently greater than 95% of the crowding standard for the past six months, mostly on trips serving students near UTSC. Adding trips in that period would bring crowding back within the standard.

Resources
The added trips can be run with existing equipment and within the adopted budget.
