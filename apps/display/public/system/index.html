How Transit Agents works

Agents do the first pass of a transit study. Everything they add goes through one service that checks it, keeps it, and leaves every decision to a named planner.

The Transit Agents service

1. The checks at the door Before anything goes in:
  • No source, no number
  • A quote must really be on the page it cites
  • Only a named planner can decide
  • Visitors share a capped daily budget, in their own sandbox
2. One shared record Every document, finding, render and decision, in order. Nothing in it can be quietly edited or deleted later. append-only · hash-chained
3. Views and live updates The draft study, the trail behind each number, a timeline, and the spend on AI, streamed as it changes
