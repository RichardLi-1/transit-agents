How the agents work

  1. 01

    Ask

    A planner asks in a study's chat, from Slack or from the command line.

  2. 02

    Plan

    The lead splits the request into questions, and gives each to a fresh specialist: up to 4 at once.

  3. 03

    Specialists and skills

    Each specialist takes at most one skill: its instructions, and any scripts only it may run. The lead sees only each skill's name and description.

  4. 04

    Evidence

    Every figure is cited to its page or dataset. The service rejects a figure whose quote isn't on the page, and scripts do the arithmetic.

  5. 05

    Planners decide

    Agents never decide, rate a finding or edit the log. They have no tools to.
